# Phase 8 — Synthesize & Teach

> The phase where the curriculum compresses into reusable judgment, a personal stack reference, and public artefacts. The goal is not to "finish" — it's to produce the artefacts that survive the next wave of the field.

## Build deliverable

Three artefacts that compress what the curriculum produced:

1. **The "When To Use Multi-Agent" decision tree** — every branch defended, anchored in real numbers from your Phase 4 / Phase 5 measurements. Lives at [[When To Use Multi-Agent]] in the Obsidian vault; mirror in `decision_tree/README.md` here.
2. **Personal agent stack reference** — the layered substrate (models, frameworks, patterns, ops, security) with your specific defaults and the conditions under which you'd swap each layer. Lives at [[Agent Stack — Personal Reference]] in the vault; mirror in `stack_reference/README.md` here.
3. **Public post series for `asleekgeek.com`** — 4–6 posts distilled from the build work. Drafts in `posts/`, ready for review and publish.

## Discipline

The single most important Phase 8 habit:

> After every agent project — even the ones that worked — write a three-sentence post-mortem note: what surprised you, what pattern didn't fit, what you'd do differently. Add it to a `Post-Mortems` index in the vault. Six months in, that index is the most-read note in the vault.
